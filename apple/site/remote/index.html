Control

The whole collection.
From the sofa.

Everything the Mac can play, a tap away. Search for a title or a composer, or just say what you're in the mood for and get a playlist. The music keeps playing on the Mac's speakers; the phone is the remote.

  • Play, pause, skip and scrub, with the Mac answering at once

  • Your playlists, favorites and recently played, from the Mac
  • Lock Screen and Control Center, without opening the app

Now Playing

The visualizers,
a screen away from your eyes.

Now Playing fills the screen with the same visualizers as the Mac, drawn on your phone or iPad and moving with the music. Swipe to change. On an iPad by the TV, it's the best seat in the house.

Listen

Take the music
to another room.

Tap Listen, and the sound moves from the Mac to your iPhone or iPad over your own Wi-Fi. The Mac goes quiet, your headphones take over, and the visualizer stays in step with what you hear. Put the phone away; it keeps playing.

  • Studio-quality Opus, 256 kbit/s
  • Encrypted
  • Never leaves your network
  • Plays with the phone locked

Discover by sound

Love this one?
Hop to the next by how it sounds.

Resonance knows how every tune sounds, from a fingerprint it takes of each one. Ask for the tunes that sound like the one playing, pick one, and ask again. It's the best way to wander through 60,000 tunes, and now it fits in one hand.

  • Sounds Like from Now Playing or any tune in a list
  • The story behind a tune, where its composer left one

Sharing

Found a good one?
Send it.

Share any tune from the phone. In Messages it shows up with its title, composer and a picture of how it sounds. Your friend taps it and it plays in Resonance, or they get a page that shows them where to find it. Links friends send you open in the Remote.

Getting started

Paired in seconds.
Private by design.

  1. 1

    Turn on Remote

    In Resonance on your Mac, open Settings, then Remote. It shows a QR code.

  2. 2

    Scan it

    Point the Remote, or the iPhone's camera, at the code. That's the whole pairing.

  3. 3

    Play

    From then on the Remote finds your Mac by itself whenever it's nearby.

Stays at home

The phone talks to your Mac directly, over your own network. No cloud, no account, no server in between.

Encrypted

Commands and sound are encrypted with a key only your Mac and your phone hold.

Yours to remove

The Mac lists every paired device. Remove one, and it can't connect again without a new pairing.

Questions

Good to know

Do I need a Mac?

Yes. The Remote plays what Resonance on your Mac plays: the music, the collection and the fingerprints all live there. The phone controls it, shows it, and can play its sound.

Does it work away from home?

It works wherever your phone can reach your Mac directly: on the same Wi-Fi, or close by without one. It doesn't go over the internet.

Will it play through my headphones?

With Listen, yes: the sound comes to the phone and plays through whatever it's connected to, and the Mac goes quiet. Without Listen, the Mac keeps playing on its own speakers.

What music does it play?

The music of the Commodore 64: all 60,000 tunes of the High Voltage SID Collection, the home computer's soundtrack from the 1980s to today. More machines are planned for Resonance.

Does it work on iPad?

Yes, with a layout made for the bigger screen: a sidebar, the library beside Now Playing, and the visualizers full screen.
