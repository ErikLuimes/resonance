## Share a tune

Send a friend the tune you're listening to.

- **Share…** next to Add to… in Now Playing, the inspector and the menu bar player, and **Share** in a tune's right-click menu, makes a short link to any tune in HVSC: Copy Link, Messages, Mail, or any other app.
- Pasted into Messages, Slack, Discord and the like, the link shows the tune's card: title, composer, year, length and its sound fingerprint.
- A click on **Open in Resonance** opens the tune in Resonance, at the same subtune. If your collection is older and doesn't have it, Resonance offers the HVSC update; if you have no collection yet, the download.
- Friends without Resonance get a page with the tune's card, its fingerprint turning, and a download link.
