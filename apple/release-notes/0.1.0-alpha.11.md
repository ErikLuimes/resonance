## A new look for the terminal app

`resonance` in Terminal got a makeover, in the style of an old BBS.

- **Your accent color.** The terminal app now uses the accent you chose in System Settings, and only for what's chosen or playing: a ● on the tune that plays, a ❯ at the cursor, and the played part of the bar.
- **Sections** sit on gray bars, with thin lines between the columns that fade out toward the bottom.
- **Tidier lists.** Composer names get the room they need, lengths line up on the right, and long playlist names shorten instead of hiding their count.
- **Fewer keys at the bottom**, only the ones you use most. `?` still has them all.
- `resonance pick` lists tunes the same way.

## Scrub from the terminal

Press `t` to pick a point in the tune with the arrow keys, or jump to a tenth of it with a number, then `Enter` to go there. As in the app, the bar fills in gray to where you're going while the playhead catches up. It works on the reSIDfp and SIDLite direct engines.

## And more

- My Playlist is in the terminal app now, after Favorites.
- Playlists you change in the app update in the terminal app right away, without restarting it.
- A tune whose length isn't known gets a bar that sweeps across, instead of one that stays empty.
