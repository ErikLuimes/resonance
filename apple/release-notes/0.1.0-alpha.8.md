## Simpler engine settings

Settings now offers two engines: reSIDfp, the most accurate, and SIDLite direct, the lightest. Both can scrub.

The older SIDLite and reSID engines are gone from Settings, along with Widen mono tunes, which only worked on them.

## And more

- Security updates for the libraries behind downloads and the terminal app.
- The Settings sidebar is wide enough for its titles.
