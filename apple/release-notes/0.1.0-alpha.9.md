## Crash reports that stay on your Mac

If Resonance quits unexpectedly, it now saves a report, and Settings → Crashes lists them.

- **Nothing is sent.** A report only leaves your Mac when you copy it, save it or choose **Report on GitHub…**.
- **Cleaned first.** The copy you share has your user name, your Mac's name and file paths taken out. GitHub issues are public, so read it before you post.
- **Yours to delete**, one at a time or all at once.

## And more

- About Resonance has a desk now. Switch the monitor on and look around: the Credits moved there from the Help menu.
- The window's columns hold still while you resize it, after a crash during a resize.
- The terminal app opens straight into the player, without the logo screen.
