## Eight new visualizers

Now Playing has room for three at a time, and you choose which in **Settings → Appearance**, from pictures of each.

- **Notes**: the tune as sheet music, with a ball hopping from note to note.
- **Spectrum**: an analyzer with each voice's curve, its peaks and its notes.
- **Terrain**: the last few seconds of the spectrum as a landscape.
- **Ink**: each voice pours dye into dark, swirling water.
- **Liquid Light**: glossy oil on a projector's glass, like a 1960s light show.
- **Ferrofluid**: black liquid that stands in spikes where a voice sounds.
- **Silicon**: the tune's SID in 3D with the lid off, its real die at work.
- **Nimbus**: balls of cloud that merge, swell and glow with each voice.

## And more

- Playing a tune takes far less of your Mac: about 15% CPU with the window showing, down from about 47%.
- Pausing, stopping, skipping, seeking and quitting fade out instead of clicking.
