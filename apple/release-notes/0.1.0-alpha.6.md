## A fresh start

This version is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app, without a trip to Privacy & Security. It also runs in the App Sandbox, macOS's safe room for apps, and that means a fresh start: Resonance opens on Welcome again and doesn't see your old favourites, playlists or settings. Your earlier files are still in ~/Library/Application Support/Resonance. To keep the collection you already have, choose its HVSC folder there when Welcome asks, instead of downloading it again.

## Smart Playlists

Say what you'd like to hear, in your own words, and Resonance builds a playlist from all of HVSC by how each tune actually sounds.

## Resonance, from anywhere on your Mac

- Siri and Shortcuts: “Surprise me”, “Play something like this”, “Drift from this tune”, “Favourite this tune” and “Play Favourites”.
- Spotlight finds the collection's tunes, and in Finder Space shows a SID file's title, composer and release.
- The Dock icon shows a download's or scan's progress, and its menu has the controls. When Resonance is in the background, a finished download tells you.
- The `resonance` command and its terminal app: playlists, search, Sounds Like, the tracker and full-screen visuals. Assistants such as Claude can use it too. Install it from Settings → Integrations.
- A shared tune link now opens Resonance directly.

## And more

- Search is a place of its own: type from anywhere.
- Under the Hood (⌥⌘U) slides up the tracker, registers and sweeps, every chip at once for multi-SID tunes.
- Tunes outside HVSC get their length worked out, shown with a ~.
- Now Playing shows the tune's fingerprint sphere.
- Press ? for the keyboard shortcuts, with Vim and Emacs keys in Settings.
- New credits: About Resonance → Credits.
