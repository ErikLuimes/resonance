  TITLE: Scotland the Brave
 ARTIST: Traditional
COMMENT: This was one of six tunes that featured in a music demo produced by
         Peter Clarke and Paul Hughes in 1986. The tunes eventually were
         released in the 2025 demo For Pete's Sake as a posthumous tribute to
         Peter Clarke.
         "Ooh Er! How bad will this be...? Yep! Pretty bad.. I guess I was
         focusing on the drums, trying to get a military beat.." (PC)
